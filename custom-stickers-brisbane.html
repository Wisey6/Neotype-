What we print

Three cuts, six finishes, one minimum of fifteen per design.

Die-cut stickers

Cut to the exact contour of your artwork, with a clean border that follows every curve. The one most Brisbane cafés, breweries and bands order for merch and packaging.

Kiss-cut stickers

Cut through the vinyl but not the backing, so the sticker peels off a square sheet. Easier to hand out at markets and easier to post in an envelope.

Sticker sheets

Several designs kiss-cut onto one sheet. Good value when you want a set rather than a single design — popular with illustrators and small brands.

Finishes

All on waterproof, UV-resistant vinyl rated for five-plus years outdoors — Queensland sun included.

  • Matte — flat, no glare, takes pen. The default, and the best value.
  • Gloss — sharp colour and contrast, wipes clean.
  • Satin — between the two, a soft sheen.
  • Holographic — rainbow shift in the light. The most-ordered upgrade.
  • Glitter — textured sparkle in the laminate.
  • Chrome — mirror-metallic, the premium option.
  • Clear — transparent vinyl, no white border, for glass and windows.
